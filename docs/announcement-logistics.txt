HUSS HQ — Logistics & Command Update

Stockpiles and logistics orders are now available!

• Track public and reserve stockpiles, counted inventory, reserved stock and reported expiry timers with /stockpile.
• Create production, transport and frontline supply orders with /order. Claim work, record pickups, make partial deliveries and return unused goods.
• Reserve access codes are restricted to logistics staff. Inventory and timers are manually reported — the bot does not read or control Foxhole stockpiles.
• Find the new Logistics overview in /botpanel.

Command improvements:
• Only Officers and Commanders can promote: Officers up to Hussar, Commanders up to Officer.
• One eligible warning expires automatically after 30 days, with a 30-day interval between removals. Officer voting has been retired.
• New tools: /memberinfo, /setup-check, /certify, /warreport and /auditlog.
• Safer resets, persistent activity-check deadlines, full paginated ticket transcripts and improved event forms.

Use /order list to find logistics work. Ask an Officer if you need help getting started.
