# HUSS HQ — War hub and member tools

## Available this war

**Members**
- `/onboarding view`: your verification, interests, squad, training and first-operation checklist.
- `/hub view`: find orders, accessible stockpiles, recruiting squads and upcoming events in one place.
- `/hub reminders`: choose operation-channel reminders, DMs or no personal reminder.
- Reminder buttons: **Confirm attendance**, **Running late**, **Withdraw**.
- `/feedback submit`: send a private bug report or suggestion and track its status.
- `/absence set`: tell officers when you expect to return. Activity-check requirements still apply.

**Officers and Commanders**
- `/hub panel`: post a shared entry point that opens private member views.
- `/feedback queue` and `/feedback update`: investigate reports and communicate progress.
- `/absence list`: review notices alongside the Commander rollover preview.
- `/healthalerts status`: grouped error alerts and recovery notices in the officer channel.

## Prepared for next war — 🕒

- Officer-approved operation debriefs and lessons.
- Squad progress reviews and approved next-war goals.
- Notification preferences and private update inbox.
- War archive for summaries, goals, operations, awards and debriefs.

These workflows remain locked until the next-war release. Existing approval rules and visibility restrictions continue to apply. Operation RSVPs do not automatically award qualifications or commendations.
